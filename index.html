ABOUT /

As a Lead Product Designer with fifteen years of experience, I build design systems for products where money is involved — banking, trading, payments. What I enjoy most sits between strategy and the screen: working out what a business actually needs, then what the product has to do about it, and being able to explain both to the people paying for it.

Most of my work lives at two ends of the same product. There is the first time someone opens something they do not understand yet, where the interface has to teach before it can help. And there is the thousandth time, when the same person is checking one number on the way to work. Designing for both is harder than it sounds, because they want opposite things — and a good system is usually what keeps the peace between them.

I spent four years at Backbase building the design language behind a white-label banking platform, and it taught me most of what I know about systems. What stayed with me is the governance side: deciding what earns a place in the system, teaching it to teams I had no authority over.

I have managed a small design team, and more often led work across teams that did not report to me, which takes persuasion rather than process. My background is industrial design and a long stretch as an art director, so I still care a great deal about typography, hierarchy and restraint. These days that sits alongside motion, accessibility and a fair amount of AI-assisted work, which has changed how I prototype more than anything else in the last few years.
