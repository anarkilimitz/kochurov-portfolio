ABOUT

As a Lead Product Designer with 13+ years of experience, I specialise in crafting scalable design systems, driving UX/UI innovation, and leading high-performing teams. I thrive at the intersection of strategy and execution, ensuring that design decisions not only enhance user experience but also align with business goals.

Throughout my career, I’ve built and refined design systems that improve workflow efficiency, foster consistency, and elevate product quality.

Beyond design execution, I focus on mentorship and team leadership, fostering a culture of collaboration where designers think beyond aesthetics to solve complex product challenges. I believe in the power of data-driven design, balancing qualitative research with real-world constraints to create solutions that are effective.

With expertise in interaction design, motion design, accessibility, and AI-driven tools, I bring a forward-thinking approach to modern product design. I excel in working cross-functionally with product managers, engineers, and stakeholders to translate vision into tangible, high-impact experiences.
