STARTING

The design language is still not fully uncovered, especially given its power for brand and product design. In Backbase, we reached this point by identifying many different problematic aspects of product design that blocked healthy growth. We decided to develop a design language that would align with the product stylistically and create powerful parallels between experience design strategies and approaches.

ROLE

My role in this team was around mostly visual aspects of the product how to find a better connection with the brand and product, defining and translating brand principles to the brand, intersecting with the experience part of the product and how to bring the brand-to-product motion.
